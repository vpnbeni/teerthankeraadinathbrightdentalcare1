# Plan Upgrade - Quick Start Guide

## For Users

### How to Upgrade Your Plan

1. **Go to Payments Page**
   - Navigate to `/#/payments` in your client dashboard
   - View your current subscription status

2. **Click "Upgrade Plan"**
   - Button appears if you're not on the best plan
   - Located in the subscription status section

3. **Choose Your New Plan**
   - View available upgrade options
   - See how many additional sessions you'll get
   - Check the upgrade price

4. **Complete Payment**
   - Click "Upgrade Now" on your chosen plan
   - Razorpay payment window opens automatically
   - Enter payment details and confirm

5. **Enjoy More Sessions!**
   - New sessions are added immediately
   - Your subscription remains active
   - Confirmation email sent

## What Happens When You Upgrade?

### Sessions Are Added (Not Replaced!)
- **Before Upgrade**: 3 sessions remaining
- **Upgrade to 8-session plan**: +8 sessions
- **After Upgrade**: 11 sessions total ✨

### Your Subscription Period
- If still active: Dates stay the same
- If expired: New period starts
- Status: Always set to "active"

### Payment
- Secure payment via Razorpay
- Instant confirmation
- Email receipt sent

## Available Plans

Plans are shown in order from lowest to highest:
- Each plan has a specific number of sessions
- Higher plans = more sessions
- You can only upgrade to plans with MORE sessions than your current plan

## Upgrade Indicators

### You'll see upgrade options when:
✅ You're not on the highest-tier plan
✅ Your subscription is active or expired
✅ Better plans are available

### You won't see upgrade options when:
❌ You're already on the best plan
❌ No higher-tier plans exist

## Benefits of Upgrading

🎯 **More Sessions**: Get additional dental care sessions
💰 **Better Value**: Higher plans often have better per-session value
⭐ **Premium Features**: Access to additional benefits
🚀 **Priority Support**: Enhanced customer service

## Need Help?

- Check your current plan in the Payments page
- View payment history in the "Payment History" tab
- Contact support if you have questions
